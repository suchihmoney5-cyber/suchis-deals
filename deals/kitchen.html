Kitchen roundup

Editorial notes on cookware and prep tools — framed around features and fit, not fabricated “I used this daily” claims.

A useful kitchen usually grows from a few durable basics: a solid skillet, a sharp knife you will maintain, and storage that keeps ingredients visible. Below are category-style picks with what to look for when comparing listings.

Cookware

Seasoned cast-iron skillet

Strong heat retention for searing and oven finishes. Compare diameter (10–12 in covers most households), pour spouts, and whether a lid is included. Expect a learning curve on seasoning and hand-washing — not ideal if you need fully dishwasher-safe nonstick for every meal.
