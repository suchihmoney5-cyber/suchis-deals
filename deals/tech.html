Everyday tech roundup

Accessories where specs matter: ports, wattage, codec support, and cable length — without gadget hype.

Tech accessories fail quietly when they almost match your device. Before buying a hub or charger, note your laptop’s USB-C power delivery needs, display resolution, and whether you need data transfer or charge-only cables.

Desk

USB-C multiport hub

Expands a single USB-C port into HDMI, USB-A, and sometimes Ethernet or SD. Confirm HDMI version for 4K/60Hz if you need it, and whether the hub supports power delivery passthrough so your laptop can charge while docked. Cheap hubs may throttle under multi-display load.

Over-ear wireless headphones

For commute or focus blocks, compare ANC strength, multipoint Bluetooth, and battery claims at realistic volumes. Comfort after 1–2 hours matters more than peak marketing hours. If you need wired failover, confirm a 3.5 mm option.

Power

GaN USB-C wall charger (65W-class)

GaN chargers shrink brick size for laptop-capable wattage. Match wattage to your device’s requirements and prefer models with clear PPS/PD profiles. Multi-port units split power — read the allocation chart so two devices do not undercharge.

Cables

USB-C to USB-C cable (100W-rated)

Not all USB-C cables support high-wattage charging or high-speed data. Look for e-marker chips on higher-power cables and length you will actually use (1–2 m). Buy from listings that state both power and data ratings explicitly.
