Privacy Policy

How this static site handles information. Last updated: September 2026.

Overview

Suchi’s Deals (“we,” “us”) operates a largely static website. We aim to collect as little personal information as practical. This policy describes what may happen when you visit or contact us.

Information you provide

If you email us via the contact form (mailto) or another published address, we receive whatever you include in that message (such as your name, email address, and message content). We use that information only to respond to your inquiry.

Server & hosting logs

When you host this site on a provider (for example GitHub Pages, Netlify, or Cloudflare Pages), that provider may automatically log technical data such as IP address, browser type, dates/times of requests, and referring URLs. Those logs are governed by the host’s privacy policy. We do not operate a custom backend that stores visitor accounts.

Cookies & analytics

The default site files do not set advertising cookies. If you later add analytics (such as a privacy-oriented analytics script) or embedded third-party content, update this page to name those tools and link to their policies.

Affiliate links (Amazon)

Links to Amazon may include our Associates tracking ID (suchisdeals0e-20) or short URLs provided by Amazon. When you follow those links, Amazon may set cookies and process data under Amazon’s privacy policy. We do not receive your full payment details from Amazon purchases — only aggregated affiliate reporting typical of the Associates Program.

Third-party fonts

Pages may load fonts from Google Fonts. Google may receive your IP address as part of serving those assets. You can self-host fonts later if you prefer to avoid that request.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy when the site’s practices change. The “Last updated” date above will change accordingly.

Privacy questions: use the Contact page or email the address listed there.
